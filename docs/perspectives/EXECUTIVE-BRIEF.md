## The AI Paradox

Your organization invested in AI development tools. Your engineers generate code 10-50x faster than before. But your time-to-market improved only 1.5-2x.

The missing 8x is lost to a comprehension bottleneck:

```
                    BEFORE AI          AFTER AI
  Creation speed:   ██                 ████████████████████  (10-50x)
  Search speed:     ██                 ██                    (1x, unchanged)
  Shipping speed:   ██                 ███                   (1.5x realized)

  THE GAP: Your team creates at AI speed but navigates at human speed.
```

**The cost is concrete.** If your 20-person engineering team spends 40% of their time searching for context, tracing dependencies, and switching between tools, that is 8 full-time equivalent salaries spent on retrieval instead of creation. AI made this worse, not better -- there is now 10x more output to search through.
